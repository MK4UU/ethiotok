Respect the rights of creators and rights holders.

Reporting infringement

To report content that you believe infringes your copyright or other intellectual-property rights, email ethiotoklive@gmail.com.

Include

  • Your name and contact information.
  • A description of the protected work.
  • The EthioTok username, video, profile, or content location involved.
  • A good-faith statement explaining the claimed infringement.
  • A statement that the information is accurate and that you are authorized to act.

Misuse

Submitting knowingly false or misleading reports may have legal consequences.
