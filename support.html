How can we help?

Find answers, report a problem, or contact EthioTok support.

Account and login

Provide your username and registered email. Never send your password.

Video or LIVE issue

Include device model, Android version, app version, issue time, and screenshots.

Safety report

Use in-app reporting where available. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Account deletion

Review the deletion page and submit a request using the email connected to your account.

Privacy request

Request access, correction, or deletion of personal information where applicable.

Business inquiry

Contact EthioTok for partnerships, creator programs, or platform inquiries.
