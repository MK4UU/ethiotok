EthioTok (“EthioTok,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides a social video and live-streaming service. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, disclosed, retained, and protected when you use the EthioTok application, website, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data such as email address, username, display name, profile image, biography, and authentication records.
  • User content including videos, images, captions, comments, chat messages, live streams, guest interactions, and reports.
  • Device, app, usage, diagnostics, crash, security, notification, and performance information.
  • Camera, microphone, photos, videos, or files when you grant permission.

2. How We Use Information

  • Operate accounts, profiles, feeds, messaging, live streaming, PK battles, virtual gifts, notifications, and social features.
  • Store, display, transmit, personalize, secure, and moderate content.
  • Improve performance, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Service Providers

EthioTok may use providers for authentication, databases, cloud storage, messaging, diagnostics, moderation, analytics, hosting, notifications, and live-streaming infrastructure, including Google Firebase services where enabled.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared through public features, with service providers acting for EthioTok, when required by law, or in connection with a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

5. Public Content and LIVE

Profiles, public videos, live streams, comments, usernames, gift activity, rankings, and similar interactions may be visible to other users.

6. Retention

Information is retained as needed to provide the service, maintain safety, comply with law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce agreements. Limited backup, transaction, or security records may remain after deletion.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain certain information. Visit the Account Deletion page or email ethiotoklive@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

EthioTok is not intended for children below the minimum age required by applicable law.

10. International Processing

Information may be processed outside your country with safeguards where required by applicable law.

11. Changes

We may update this policy and will publish the revised version with a new effective date.
