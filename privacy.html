EthioTok Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

EthioTok respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the EthioTok mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Account details such as email address, username, display name, and profile photo.
  • User-generated content such as videos, images, comments, chat messages, live streams, and profile information.
  • Technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, IP address, and crash data.
  • Activity information such as likes, follows, shares, gifts, viewing activity, and interactions with other users.
  • Notification tokens used to send push notifications.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide video sharing, live streaming, chat, PK battles, gifts, and social features.
  • Store and display user content.
  • Improve app performance, security, and reliability.
  • Send service-related notifications.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, spam, and unauthorized access.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. Firebase and Third-Party Services

EthioTok uses Google Firebase services, which may include Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, Cloud Functions, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and crash or performance services.

These services may process technical and account-related data according to Google's privacy practices.

Content you upload or publish may be visible to other users depending on the feature and your account settings. You are responsible for the content you post and for ensuring that you have the right to share it.

5. Live Streams and Chat

Live streams, comments, chat messages, PK battles, and guest interactions may be visible to other users in real time. These interactions may be stored for moderation, safety, technical support, or service improvement purposes.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only when necessary with service providers, for legal compliance, to protect users and the service, or as part of a business transfer.

7. Data Security

8. Data Retention
