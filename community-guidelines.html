Community Standards

Community Guidelines

Standards for respectful, creative, and safe participation.

Respect people

No bullying, threats, targeted harassment, hateful conduct, or degrading attacks.

Protect minors

Content that exploits, sexualizes, grooms, or endangers minors is prohibited.

No violent harm

Do not promote credible threats, terrorism, graphic abuse, or serious harmful acts.

Be authentic

No scams, impersonation, coordinated manipulation, or fake engagement.

Respect rights

Only share content you have the right to use. Respect privacy and intellectual property.

Use LIVE responsibly

Hosts, guests, moderators, and viewers must follow these rules during live streams.

Enforcement

Depending on severity, EthioTok may limit distribution, remove content, restrict features, suspend accounts, terminate accounts, preserve evidence, or report conduct where legally required.

Reporting

Use in-app reporting tools where available or email ethiotoklive@gmail.com.
