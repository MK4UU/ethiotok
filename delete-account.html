Delete Your Account

Request deletion of your EthioTok account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Email ethiotoklive@gmail.com from the address connected to your account.
  2. Use the subject: EthioTok Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your username and display name. Do not send your password.
  4. EthioTok may request verification of account ownership.

What will be deleted

After verification, EthioTok will delete or de-identify account information and associated personal data that is not required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, transaction, or legitimate operational purposes.
