FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about accounts, content, privacy, and support.

What is EthioTok?

A social video and live-streaming platform designed for Ethiopian creators and communities.

How do I report content or a user?

Use the in-app reporting option where available or contact support with the relevant username, content, and details.

How do I delete my account?

Use the in-app deletion option when available and visit the Delete Account page for the web request process.

Does EthioTok sell personal data?

EthioTok's Privacy Policy states that personal information is not sold.

How do I contact support?

Email ethiotoklive@gmail.com.
